How to Play

  • Drag to move and tap to drop. On a keyboard, use the left and right arrows and Space.
  • Two of the same kind merge (2 → 1, frees space!)
  • Scores grow exponentially: Kitten 10pts → Savannah 10,000pts
  • Two Savannahs touch → vanish + 20,000pt bonus (field clear!)
  • Too long above the red line = game over
