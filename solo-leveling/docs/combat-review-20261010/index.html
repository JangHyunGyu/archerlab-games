Findings and corrections

  • Short casts used fixed 20 fps body animations: a 190 ms action could end before recovery. Each action now plays all six frames within its requested duration, including Phaser's required null frame-rate override.
  • Arc skills lacked a body attack trigger. The shared arc implementation now starts the character's directional motion; piercing skills honor their configured duration.
  • Sword and claw trails stayed at the starting position while the player moved. Their origins now follow the player. The sword trail also retains brightness at contact.
  • Fifteen Higgsfield strips replace the five characters' three authored attack directions. Left is an exact mirror of right. Source-camera scale is calibrated before the common frame fit; crouching does not independently resize the body.
  • The first frontal mage candidate changed staff hands at contact and was rejected. A replacement keeps one staff hand; each cell is mirrored separately to match the established idle artwork without reversing the temporal sequence.
  • Six effects were regenerated as genuine six-cell sequences: shadow slash, ruler pressure, fear, tiger quake, tiger guard, and flame arc. Fixed cell scale and pivot preserve ignition, growth, contact, breakup and dissipation.
  • Character glow and movement trails use the character accent. Root motion is restrained for casters. Reduced motion disables cosmetic movement afterimages and attack-root deformation.
