What is Archerlab Games? A free browser game portal with no download

Archerlab Games (game.archerlab.dev) is a free web game portal that collects 10 casual games made by developer Hyungyu Jang. From potion sorting and falling blocks to merge puzzles, match-3, defense and online volleyball, you can play on mobile or desktop browsers without installing or downloading anything.

Who it is for

  • Players looking for light puzzle games for short sessions
  • People who want to play on a phone or PC without installing an app
  • Fans of casual games with scores and records to chase

How to use it

  1. Choose a game card on the portal.
  2. When the game opens, follow the on-screen instructions. No install or login is needed.
  3. Use touch on mobile, or mouse and keyboard on desktop. Controls vary by game and are explained on screen.

Features

  • 10 free browser games in one place
  • Short rounds across puzzle, merge, defense and sports genres
  • Mobile and desktop support
  • Score challenges in some games, such as a hall of fame

Good to know

Rules and controls differ by game and are explained on each game screen. A current browser gives the most stable experience.

Frequently asked questions

Is Archerlab Games free?

Yes. Every game on the portal is free to play.

Do I need to install or sign up?

No. Start playing directly in the browser.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, on both mobile and desktop browsers.

Which games are there?

Bubbly Lab, Lumen Shift, Jelly Pang!, Parking Escape, Jewelria, School Zombie Defense, Blockpang, Shadow Survival, Cat Tower and Slime Volley.

Is there an English version?

The portal is mainly Korean and some games support English.

Where should I start?

Bubbly Lab has 60-second stages for quick sessions. Cat Tower and Jelly Pang! suit merge-puzzle fans.
