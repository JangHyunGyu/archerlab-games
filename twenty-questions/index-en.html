How It Works

How do I play Korean Semantle?

Every guess returns a semantic similarity score (-100 to +100). If your guess is among the 1,000 closest words to the secret, its exact rank also appears.

How does the live timer & leaderboard work?

The stopwatch anchors to server time the moment a round starts and keeps counting even when minimized or closed. Verified solves with 2+ guesses compete for the fastest Hall of Fame clear time.

Why can antonyms score high?

Vectors are learned from Wikipedia and Common Crawl contexts, so words appearing in similar sentences (like "사랑" love and "증오" hate) share high scores.

Is there a daily limit?

No. Play unlimited rounds back to back anytime.
