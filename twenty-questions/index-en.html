How It Works

How do I play Semantle?

Every guess returns a semantic similarity score (-100 to +100). If your guess is among the 1,000 closest words to the secret, its exact rank also appears.

When do rounds reset and how is time measured?

Every 6 hours (00:00, 06:00, 12:00, 18:00 KST / 15:00, 21:00, 03:00, 09:00 UTC), everyone resets to the same new word and guess history clears. You can toggle the unified ranking between Most Solved and Fastest Time from round open (2+ guesses). Submitting the same name merges unique solved rounds and keeps your fastest time.

Why can antonyms score high?

Similarity reflects how often words appear in related contexts, so opposites used in the same situations (like "love" and "hate") can both score high.

Can I give up? Where do I check previous answers?

There is no give-up during a live round so everyone competes fairly. As soon as the 6-hour countdown finishes, the previous round’s answer and Top 1,000 words unlock at the top bar and lobby.
